# Public disclosure boundary

This repository is a clean-room portfolio artifact informed by generic measurement-system requirements. It is not a sanitized distribution of an employer or client codebase.

## Deliberately excluded

- employer, customer, supplier, or product names;
- real serial numbers, production orders, workstation identities, or network addresses;
- chip identities, firmware versions, proprietary commands, register maps, or packet examples;
- proprietary calibration, compensation, filtering, or control algorithms;
- real calibration coefficients, pass/fail thresholds, power limits, or customer measurements;
- original technical agreements, communication manuals, screenshots, photographs, archives, or CSV files.

## Public substitutions

- devices and channels use neutral codenames;
- Protocol-X is independently invented and documented in this repository;
- all committed measurements are generated by a seeded simulator;
- policy limits are demonstrator values with no claimed product meaning;
- diagrams and plots are generated from public code, not edited from production screenshots.

## Contribution claim

The appropriate claim is the ability to design a traceable acquisition-to-validation workflow, implement defensive protocol parsing, build reproducible experiments, and communicate evidence boundaries. The repository must not be cited as evidence of a named product's specifications, customer deployment, or certified accuracy.
